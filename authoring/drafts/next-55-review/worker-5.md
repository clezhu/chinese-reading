Contextual choices reviewed:

- **大夫 dài fu** means doctor, not imperial official dà fū; corrected this occurrence during language review.
- **数 shǔ** counts gifts/photos; **调 tiáo** adjusts sound/light; **转 zhuǎn** changes direction; **参 cān** participates; **当真 dàng zhēn** takes something seriously.
- **血 xiě** is conversational literal blood; **心血 xīn xuè** is figurative effort. **精神 jīng shen** is vitality here. **睡着 shuì zháo** differs from 睡觉 shuì jiào.
- The archive's 弄混 entry has a reading distinction from the general 混 entry. Rephrased those passages as explicit **混在一起 hùn zài yì qǐ**, supported by the single-character mixing sense, rather than silently treating that compound entry as hùn.
- Neutral tones include the repeated syllables in family terms 姐姐/哥哥/弟弟, 衣服 yī fu, 什么 shén me, 看看 kàn kan, 嘛 ma and 啦 la. The experienced-action **过 guo** in 看过 was checked separately from lexical 过 guò. **拜拜 bái bái** is farewell; **拜托 bài tuō** is the request expression.
- Reviewed **得 de / dé / děi**, **乐 lè / yuè**, **长 zhǎng** in 队长, **重 zhòng / chóng** in importance versus 重新, and **为 wèi / wéi** in purpose versus 代为. Applied the repo's 一/不 surface-tone convention and retained conventional third-tone spelling.
