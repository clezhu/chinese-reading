## Language and target self-review

Read the completed Chinese paragraph by paragraph, revised unclear resolutions,
removed outside-bank words and extra narration, checked contextual meanings and
pinyin, and reran the canonical checker after the edits. The following are my
author assessments, not independent Mandarin approval or learner testing.

- learning-51: a missing-watch search resolves when the watch is on its owner's
  wrist. 位 introduces people; 表 is a watch; 刚 marks recent arrival; 希望 is
  the wish to recover it; 查 is the search. Due characters describe the helpers,
  phone inquiry and thanks. The lightly comic, forgetful-owner premise is intentional.
- learning-52: a boy leaves a scholar's event and encounters someone trying to
  return an object. 拉, 边, 或, 将 and 男 serve the action, place, alternatives,
  future wish and characters. His father gives a concrete identity check before
  accepting an object. 安尔 and 马克 are fictional names, not introduced vocabulary.
- learning-53: a new event worker clarifies a foreign visitor's work certificate
  and delivery arrangements by calling him. 准, 变, 证, 物 and 员 belong to the
  uncertainty, changed schedule, certificate, belongings and staff role. The
  resolution now explicitly includes the certificate as well as his belongings.
- learning-54: coworkers recover documents and a phone after separating their
  storage locations, then improve record keeping. 总, 噢, 系, 几 and 管 occur in
  repeated forgetfulness, realization, connection, item count and management.
- learning-55: a fictional public-princess-and-killer game causes discomfort when
  a staff member assumes a newcomer has agreed to play. 杀 is a game role, not
  an actual attack. 玩, 处, 办, 主 and 气 are used in the game, handling the
  misunderstanding, organizing, princess/manager roles and anger. The ending
  gives the newcomer room to read the explanation before deciding.
- learning-56: an extra guest reveals a food miscount; smaller portions let
  everyone share. 每, 少, 切, 失 and 算 drive this small problem and resolution.
  死心 is the idiom for giving up, not literal death. 美好 describes the boy's
  enjoyment of a shared meal, rather than a detached list of pleasant words.
- learning-57: someone waits with a found work certificate, and its owner learns
  from that kindness. 性 is in 重要性, 此 in 为此, 必 in 不必, 备 in 准备 and 合
  in 合作. 主动等待 and giving up a little play time support the final reflection.
- learning-58: a recovering team leader mistakes a request to try first for
  exclusion. 德 identifies his origin, 队 his team role, 试 the repeated attempt,
  抱 his carried things and 医 the doctor. The scene gives no medical regimen;
  its point is the misunderstanding and a calmer conversation.
- learning-59: a music enthusiast helps set up an event in exchange for admission.
  通 describes achieving the setup through cooperation; 体 is in 身体, 乐 in
  乐队, 并 in the boy's response and 三 in the requested helper count. The
  setup and admission are a concrete outcome, rather than a vocabulary list.
- learning-60: an early arrival and an incorrect event time lead to calling the
  other attendees. 早, 门, 害, 歉 and 选 are the early time, closed door, the
  consequence, apology and selection event. The boy acts on his concern that
  others should not wait unnecessarily; 身体力行 is intentional idiomatic reuse.
- revision-60: a complete, longer event-preparation story: closed door, corrected
  schedule, missing helpers, cooperation, item placement, shared safety contact
  and a corrected notice. All latest-50 targets recur within this single
  situation. The organizer changes what he does after apologizing. The longer
  passage uses paragraph and sentence boundaries to keep each exchange readable.
