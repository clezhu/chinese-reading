## Language and meaningful reuse

These are original small situations, self-reviewed by the author rather than independently reviewed or learner-tested. I reviewed sentence meaning, the actions connecting each situation, every mandatory target's use, contextual pronunciation, paragraph and sentence boundaries, and help load. Early drafts included unavailable everyday characters; I changed the wording and situations rather than changing the allowed bank. Subsequent review corrected awkward substitutions and removed several unnecessary epilogues.

- **learning-81 — 会说话的蛋:** A festival team discovers a younger brother's performance using eggs and a hidden phone. 花/节 establish the event and gifts, 近 locates the discovery, 奇怪 describes the unexplained voice, and 保持好心情 is the teacher's response. Due targets serve the appointment/plan, performance space, sound source, props and delivery: 任命/合计/确认, 会场门口/教室/房间/室内, 白布/声音/消息, 蛋/装/片/警官/犯错/保护, 运/送/一度/万一. 贝斯 is an invented name; 嗨 and neutral 嗯 are spoken responses, not isolated coverage strings.
- **learning-82 — 花不是球:** A sick friend wants company for a televised match rather than another gift. 光 and 花布 furnish the room; 与 connects shared viewing; 穿 contrasts clothing with company; 愿/想象 occur in the invitation and teasing exchange. The ticket, single room, gift, medical caution, work and next visit give the due targets concrete uses. No implication that friendship treats the illness is intended; the friends describe feeling better and avoiding overexertion.
- **learning-83 — 第二次开机:** A film experiment replaces dangerous action with cloth, flowers and lighting. 影 and 第二 establish the production; 打击 is both physical action and discouragement; 使用/使 describe the safe effect; 喝水 follows the setback. 警官 and a 女士 supply natural 警/士 uses. The difficult scene, fallen cloth, father's rescue role and final reaction connect the due targets to the filmmaking problem. 火 is an imitation in the scene.
- **learning-84 — 停下来的声音:** A musician discovers that an unamplified, quieter performance works better. 月光/乐器/警察/节制/嘛 are part of the setting, intervention and reflection. The second event, card, message, alternative plan, white cloth, repeated opportunity and protective request cover the due targets. The instrument is intentionally unspecified; its acoustic sound remains after the amplification is switched off.
- **learning-85 — 海边的约定:** A couple helps a friend attend their seaside wedding and performance. 哈哈 is laughter; 帮助, 到达, 海边 and 期待/等待 are central actions rather than unrelated examples. The card's grid, gold flowers, eye problem, ride, entrance and future film appointment motivate reuse. 尼克 is an invented name. 克制 is used as affectionate advice to the excited couple.
- **learning-86 — 没有到达的信:** A child restricted to staying indoors sends a recognizable message to a friend. 托/写 drive the action; 世界 is what he misses; 除了 and 绝对 express the request. The missing identification, phone number, handmade egg, photographs, visit and improvised police role integrate the due targets. The final card is a practical attempt to avoid another failed delivery; the egg is a recognizable handmade prop, not a new character definition.
- **learning-87 — 上帝的水:** An actor playing 上帝 initially bosses others around. 姐姐, 反对, 担心 and 电影公司 frame the disagreement. A report about people without drinking water changes how he treats fellow actors; he shares water and learns that a powerful role requires cooperation. Reports, relationships, stage objects and the performance's second scene carry the due targets. 上帝 is a fictional role within a play, not a factual religious claim.
- **learning-88 — 不会飞的飞机:** A film company adapts a small stage rather than requiring a working airplane. 强 contrasts strength with 强求; 自由 and 谈论 invite alternatives; 飞机 and invented 亚罗 motivate the constrained premise. The cloth, prop gun, rehearsal, police role, directions, slogan and gold band integrate reuse. 接续 stories are not required: recurring film vocabulary here is incidental to the available bank, not a claim that this batch must have continuity.
- **learning-89 — 空出来的位置:** Organizers make entrance seating available so the narrator's parents can attend a film. 电视, 母亲, 空军 and several senses of 空 are integral. 接续放 refers to a later continuation of the screening. The family conversation, imagined sea, rescue in the film, announcement, and next outing cover the due targets. 空位 is kōng wèi; the act of making a seat free is kòng chū lái.
- **learning-90 — 跑错的路:** A distracted helper sends a runner the wrong way, then corrects the directions and pays attention. 跑 and invented 阿亚 recur in the incident; 尽管 reassures, 注意 states the failure, and 弄明白 states its repair. The mother's illness, water, route cards, sport, movie choice and shared plans provide the due reuse. 接续看 is deliberately a continuation of an unfinished film, not an incorrect attempt to write 继续 with an unavailable character.
- **revision-90 — 海边的第二场电影:** A community screening becomes a shared event when a child connects with his absent father by phone. All latest-50 targets are used in the preparations, film, arrival, music lesson, phone call and next-event discussion. No introductions are added. 帝国/空军 belong to the invented film, while the real gathering supplies the remaining targets. A child's too-loud instrument explains 节制 and the second attempt; 想象/警察/海报 arise when the child's card is kept for a future event.

Per-occurrence review retained conventional third-tone spelling, used context-appropriate 一/不 sandhi and neutral particles, and distinguished musical 乐 yuè, playing 強/强 qiáng versus 强求 qiǎng, 空军/空位 kōng versus 空出来 kòng, 尽管 jǐn, long-distance running 长 cháng, and 看管 kān. 关系 uses neutral xi; 工夫 uses neutral fu. 嗯 is neutral en in the final version. Sentences with a completed quotation followed by narration were recast or separated into independent sentence arrays.

The permanent-help report is empty for every assigned story: all story Hanzi have been introduced by their respective lesson. This is a mechanical fact about the supplied curriculum order, not proof that a learner knows the words or that the prose is easy. 接续 is somewhat formal, and the unusual egg prop and invented names are deliberate story details. I do not claim independent Mandarin approval or complete lexical knowledge from a validator pass.
