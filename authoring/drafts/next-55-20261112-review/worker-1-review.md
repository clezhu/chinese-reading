| Lesson | Intended scene, reuse and reading review |

| 101 | A courtyard performance changes after wind disrupts the stage. 另 separates people/tasks; 感恩 connects the memoir with helping backstage; 统一行动、基本任务、联系 and 电视/电影 connect the organizational targets. 枪 is an acting prop; 鬼 is a child's mistaken impression of a white bag. 文亮/马文亮 is a name, not an adjective exercise. 拜拜 uses bái bái, confirmed as the farewell entry, not religious bài bai. |
| 102 | Brothers carrying books to a hospital wait at a blocked road rather than rushing through. 情况、是否、四、黑、风险 determine the decision. Police, a running dog and the hospital call are part of the same delay. 调整 is tiáo zhěng; 嗯 is èn here; 巴不得 is bā bu de. The final calculation uses the light from the doorway, consistent with the dark setting. |
| 103 | A school bag and incomplete message prompt friends to contact 米杰; his family business explains his absence. 校 is xiào, 幸运 xìng yùn, 认识 rèn shi; 行业 elsewhere is háng yè. 米 occurs both in the name and the food. 次品/打击 describe the consequence of using inferior ingredients; they do not allege actual food poisoning. The friends' imagined crime is explicitly corrected by contact with the family. |
| 104 | A sports-event injury becomes a circulating message; the father asks the service desk for facts before repeating it. 传 is chuán, 重复 chóng fù, 数量 shù liàng, 首先 shǒu xiān and 改 is message correction. Police, blood, equipment and keeping the passage open belong to the reported incident. The story gives no medical treatment instructions. Daughter's decision not to jump is respected. |
| 105 | A school reunion gift has a card with a year but no name. 忘、客、假、局面 and 美术 all matter to the search; 假 is jià in 请假, distinct from 假话 jiǎ huà elsewhere. The dog and busy gathering explain confusion. Numbers narrow the search but do not replace personal reception. 首要、重复、传达 occur in that reception, not an isolated vocabulary recap. |
| 106 | 维尼 writes a family anniversary speech from photographs, initially misreads an expression, and asks about the actual event. 永远、十岁、根据、派 and 维持 belong to the assignment and family support. 维 is used in a name and in 维持. 金表/托/保管 explain the missing smile; father corrects the fictional rejection. Surgery, fear and family care are remembered, not declared risk-free. 音乐 is yīn yuè. 精神 in these scenes denotes vitality/energy and uses jīng shen, with neutral shen. |
| 107 | A child collecting cinema tickets at a hospital lacks the agreed letter and then feels sick. 兰姐 handles both the ticket and his welfare. 票、取、烦、恶心 and 姐 form the action; 恶心 is ě xīn for nausea. The film title 帝国 is fictional; its poster explains the ticket errand. 哥哥 is gē ge, 物品 wù pǐn, 司机 sī jī, 兄弟 xiōng dì. No treatment advice is offered. |
| 108 | A fifth ticket has the wrong date. 小宝 admits his mistake; family waits during reissue. 游、糟、令、随、五 all affect the outing. 随口/随便 are suí; 令 is lìng; 五张票 is wǔ zhāng piào. The song, book and service employee's belongings situate the wait; no promise is made that honesty always prevents consequences. 强令 qiáng lìng describes an action the mother does not take. |
| 109 | A fictional TV detective performance exposes the actor's premature accusation. 大圣、正式、探案、嘴快 and 毒 are integral; the alleged poisoning is part of the script and the claim is disproved. The air-force guest provides another perspective, not military authority over a real investigation. 中了毒 is zhòng le dú. There are no actual poisoning instructions. |
| 110 | A school group attends a science centre and learns that a free entry is compatible with rules and real costs. 越…越…、自律、收费/白费、科学 and 麻烦/手麻 have contextual meanings. 麻烦 is má fan, not má fán; 科 is kē; 律 lǜ. Actors/objects in the detective activity are distinguished from actual poison. The tight hand grip is released and apologized for. |
| revision-110 | A school book exchange makes the protagonist reconsider a book he almost gives away: it preserves a message from his father's hospital stay. All latest-50 targets are reused within the same event and recollection. 整理/数量/统一/维持 are operational; 美术、科学、《大圣》 identify books; 假 is leave from work; 票 tracks exchange; 嘴/恶意 mark remembered hurt; 感恩 is explicitly not demanded by the father. 探问 is an enquiry about future readers, avoiding the intrusive shade of 探听. A short gust prompts collective action. The story ends with a phone call, not a list of targets. |

Reading choices: quantities use 数 shǔ as counting and 数 shù in 数量/数字; 量 liàng in quantities/音量, not liáng; 重 chóng in 重新/重复 and zhòng in 抓得太重. 传 chuán is transmission, never biography zhuàn. 复 fù, 校 xiào, 假 jià for leave and jiǎ for false, 恶 ě for nausea and è for evil, 令 lìng, 式 shì and 麻 má are contextual selections from the frozen lists. Neutral forms such as 朋友 péng you、孩子 hái zi、东西 dōng xi、精神 jīng shen、认识 rèn shi、意思 yì si、麻烦 má fan and 妈妈-type repeated family syllables were reviewed explicitly. 一/不 spelling follows the repo's sandhi convention; underlying third tones remain conventional. Names use normal lexical tones. Punctuation is preserved; independent utterances are independent sentence arrays.
