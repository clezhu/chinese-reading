## Language and meaningful reuse

These are my author judgements, not independent Mandarin approval. Mechanical coverage does not demonstrate meaningful use. Some due sets combine many unrelated semantic fields; I used concrete settings, objects, names and dialogue rather than listing the target characters. Recurring museum/theatre situations are deliberate independent compositions; there is no promise of stylistic uniqueness or balanced thematic variety. Constructed names (for example 奥普、雷姆、艾瑞) and the hotel name 帝国饭店 are story entities, not claims about actual people, places or events.
