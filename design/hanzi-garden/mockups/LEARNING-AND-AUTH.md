## Runtime state map and evidence

Also inspected the relevant `progress.server.ts` transitions and `app/styles.css` reader lanes. Actual content is loaded read-only through the repo's existing `authoring/scripts/load-content.mjs`; [runtime-state-samples.json](runtime-state-samples.json) records lesson 1, occurrence metadata, the saved reveal set, runtime-derived contextual cards, and the final available lesson identity. No frequency data or invented story/pinyin is used.

### Story (`phase = story`)

- Exact instruction: **Try reading your new characters inside this passage without help. If you need help, click on a character to show its pinyin. Double underlined characters are unseen characters and have pinyin on by default - you do not need to know them yet.** This is presentation wording only; the occurrence-level/permanent-help mechanics below are unchanged.
- Entire real lesson 1 story, authored paragraph boundaries, contextual pinyin (including neutral/sandhi readings), and punctuation following its occurrence are retained. Pinyin and Hanzi occupy **two stable rows** even when help is hidden; revealing does not reflow the text. Sentence boundaries do not force extra breaks.
- Dotted underline = taught occurrence whose help is hidden; single underline = revealed taught occurrence; double underline = permanent help. A taught character reveals **that occurrence** only, stays visible for the pass and cannot be toggled off. Permanent help is already visible/noninteractive and never enters revision. No reveal-all or hide-all control.
- First preview has no taught reveals, but permanent help is visible. Second has exactly `0:0:0` (我) and `1:0:6` (的) saved. Other appearances of those same characters remain hidden; this demonstrates occurrence-level help, not character-wide reveal.
- **No reveals yet** is removed entirely from the visible design; its reserved status area stays empty. **All reveals saved.** stays visible after saved reveals. Pending/saving, retrying, refreshing, finishing and terminal/reload status behavior is retained. Finish cannot be activated while a reveal is pending, progress is refreshing, an attempt is terminal, or Finish is already in flight. Pending help remains optimistic; server confirmation is authoritative. No unsaved success celebration or silent skip of a save failure.
- `finishReading`: zero taught reveals → complete; any taught reveals → `revision-flashcards`, not completion. The saved reveal set freezes at Finish. No extra “finish summary” action/screen is added; the supplementary state sheet describes the existing transitions.

### Initial flashcards (`phase = flashcards`)

- Exact instruction on initial, confirmation and collected-revision card front/back screens: **Look at the character, think in your head what you think it is. Then click reveal. Click ’Got it’ if you got it right!** Verbose deck-mechanics explanations and the browser-only practice warning are removed from visible screens, not from the underlying behavior. Deck-recognised screens use just a brief readiness line and their existing next action.
- Example: real lesson 1 card 2 of 5, **的**, all authored readings **de / dī / dí / dì**. Front shows Hanzi only with one **Reveal** button. Revealed state adds all readings and **Got it / Have not got it** self-assessment. No skip, answer input, automatic scoring or reverse flip is invented. “Have not got it” is gentler presentation wording for runtime “Didn't get it,” the same false-answer action.
- Front Hanzi is centered both vertically and horizontally inside its complete card container (initial center 720/450; contextual center 720/494). Initial reveal shifts it modestly upwards to leave pinyin below; collected-revision reveal shifts it farther upwards for pinyin plus the frozen sentence. **The card rectangle and action positions do not move between front/back**, and no empty “answer area” makes the front look incomplete. These are static states, not a new reverse-flip mechanic.
- `answerCard`: only missed cards repeat; a clean smaller retry round leads to a full-deck confirmation. One clean round containing the entire deck is sufficient, including an initial clean round. Do not impose an extra confirmation after an already-clean full deck. The confirmation preview is the existing conditional stage, not a mandatory extra stage.
- Deck recognition is **not lesson completion**. **Continue to story** saves `deck-passed` first; show Saving while pending. A failed save retains the screen and offers retry/reload as appropriate; do not advance or erase a usable local deck.
- Existing deck progress is browser-only and attempt-scoped, optional storage; clearing/unavailable storage restarts the deck, not the saved lesson phase. This remains documented here but its browser-storage explanation is no longer visible on the mockups. No new server mastery system or sound-based correctness judgement.

### Contextual revision (`phase = revision-flashcards`)

- Runtime `revisionCards` deduplicates taught characters from saved reveals in story order using their **earliest revealed occurrence**. It is not click-order, first appearance, a generated example sentence, or an independent revision curriculum. This sample has 2 cards (我 then 的).
- Front hides readings **and context**. Back shows the character's complete reading list and its exact authored sentence with the final frozen per-occurrence help. Card 2 uses `1:0:6` and its original sentence: `你说：“这是我的妈妈，不是你的！”`.
- Permanent sentence help and the selected 的 are shown; 我 in this sentence remains hidden despite a different 我 occurrence having been revealed elsewhere. The second 的 also remains hidden. Context is read-only; no clicking to reveal more frozen sentence help.
- Same missed-round/clean-full-deck rules as initial cards. **Reread story** saves `revision-passed`, rotates the attempt and clears taught reveals, returning directly to story. Permanent help remains. It does **not** repeat the initial introduction/deck and does **not** earn completion. Repeat this revision/reread flow until a zero-reveal Finish.

### Completion (`phase = complete`)

- Normal sample: lesson 1, next lesson 2 exists → primary **Next lesson**, secondary **Back to lessons**. Final-available sample: actual lesson 11, `the-birthday-surprise`, kind revision → header **Lesson 11 · Revision**, primary **Back to lessons**, **no disabled/hidden placeholder Next button**. This is the end of currently available content, never a Module complete reward.
- A brief successful-completion celebration is a **requested presentation addition**, not evidence that it already exists in runtime. Restrained neutral confetti **falls from distributed positions across the page width and height**, not a concentrated burst at one spot or a cluster around the checkmark. Keep particles sparse and avoid obscuring text/actions. It lasts roughly 0.8–1.2 seconds, then settles to the same approved content/actions. Shown PNGs are static falling-particle poses, not animation files. Actions remain available, no blocking overlay and no automatic navigation.
- Celebrate only after successful server-confirmed zero-reveal Finish, **including revisits**. Do not retrigger on refresh/revalidation or replay an existing saved completion merely on opening the screen. The same completed-lesson UI applies to a successful revisit; first completion/tile check and later access remain permanent, last-completed is updated by existing rules.
- Reduced motion: go directly to the static settled checkmark/content/action state, with no moving particles/flip or layout motion. Both variants have the same information and choices. Milestone timestamps remain stored by runtime; this design does not introduce rewards or erase history. Their full timestamp display is omitted from the primary celebration composition as a presentation simplification.

## Sound and motion intent (no audio files/implementation)

Shared speaker button defaults on and remembers the setting, including across these screens; see [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) for stable accessible name **Sound**, pressed state and 44px hit target. Tooltips describe **Mute sound / Unmute sound**. All sound is optional and respects mute.

- Reveal/visual flip: quiet, short soft paper/tap sound, approximately 80–120ms. Reveal remains the same one-way reveal action; any flip is purely presentational.
- Got it: soft warm click/chime, around 100–160ms. Have not got it: equally quiet neutral tap, never a buzzer, harsh low “failure” note, penalty or punishment cue.
- Successful completion, including revisits: one gentle brief resolving chime, approximately 300–500ms, following confirmed success only. No music, repeating ambience, sounds on page load, unexpected autoplay speech or loud reward fanfare. Exact samples and volume remain to be chosen, not silently generated here.
- Reduced motion removes card flip motion and confetti motion; it does not independently change the remembered sound preference. Native static state changes remain understandable with sound off.

## Auth source map and requested policy changes

Inspected `app/components/auth-form.tsx`, `app/routes/login.tsx`, `app/routes/register.tsx`, `app/lib/auth.server.ts`, and the username schema in `migrations/0001_accounts.sql`.

- Existing username policy remains: 3–32 ASCII `[A-Za-z0-9_]`, no spaces, case-insensitive uniqueness (`COLLATE NOCASE UNIQUE`). Constraints are **hidden until that field is typed in**. Untouched forms show no rule hints, availability claims or subtitles. Once editing begins, show **one highest-priority broken rule per field**. Error text is concise, with a red border; no stacked checklist, no redundant subtitle or success paragraph. Red/green validation is an explicit user-requested exception to the neutral UI, and errors always have text as well as colour.
- **Permanent message space:** every field reserves the same 44px message slot in empty, pending, invalid and valid states. The label, input, subsequent input, form, buttons and footer keep identical coordinates across matching screen variants. Hide/show the message content, not the slot; no layout shifts, even when a rule wraps onto two lines. The password-valid slot remains empty.
- Username rule priority (presentation assumption): empty-after-edit → **Enter a username.**; spaces → **No spaces.**; length → **Use 3–32 characters.**; remaining invalid characters → **Use letters, numbers or _.** Only after local validity, check uniqueness. Keep the actual allowed characters ASCII, not accented/CJK letters. A taken result uses exactly **Username taken**, with red border; no “Choose another” or “Rechecked when you submit” copy.
- Username async states: **Checking availability…** with neutral border (not green), then either **✓ Username available** with green border, **Username taken** with red border, or **Could not check. Try again.** with neutral border/error text. An error or pending request is not evidence of availability and does not enable signup. After any edit, immediately invalidate the old Available state and cancel/ignore stale replies; apply a reply only to its matching current username. A final server-side uniqueness recheck is still authoritative, including races, but adds no redundant explanatory sentence to the visible form. No availability endpoint or API implementation is created here.
- Accessibility intent: associate each fixed message slot with its input via `aria-describedby`; locally invalid/taken fields expose `aria-invalid=true` and concise text, not colour alone. Pending availability uses `aria-busy=true` and a polite `role=status` announcement. Available status is politely announced only on actual confirmation. A failed check or submit conflict is conveyed as text in the same slot and announced appropriately (`role=alert` for the new error), never just a red/green colour change; a network error does not claim the username itself is invalid. Clear busy/error state on the next check without moving focus. Signup remains unavailable until current username confirmation and password validity are both present.
- Signup password **requested new policy**, not current runtime behavior: **8+ characters**, including uppercase, lowercase, number, symbol. Priority: empty-after-edit → **Enter a password.**; then **Use at least 8 characters.**, **Add an uppercase letter.**, **Add a lowercase letter.**, **Add a number.**, **Add a symbol.** Show only the first broken rule, only after editing, with red border and associated text. Once valid: **green border, no success text**, no visible checklist; remove `aria-invalid` while retaining the empty message slot. Current runtime instead shares a **12–128-character** rule between signup and login with no complexity requirement, so this remains an explicit future policy change, not a claim about implemented validation.
- Login must continue accepting valid **existing credentials**, even if they do not satisfy the newly requested signup complexity rules; do not retroactively apply signup creation checks or reject existing accounts. Stored hashes are verified unchanged. Any existing safety length cap needs separate implementation consideration; it must not be silently advertised as a newly approved signup rule here.
- Minimal forms keep username, password, one submit and one mode-switch link, with no descriptive subtitles. No DOB, gender, social signup, email collection, confirmation-password field, OAuth, remember-me control or recovery feature. Roblox is a conciseness reference only, not a feature request.
- Login footer is exactly **Contact admin at chloe.chenge.zhu@gmail.com for help.** This is contact guidance, not a promise of password reset/account recovery. Signup keeps the existing message unchanged: **No account recovery. We do not collect email addresses.** / **Keep your username and password safe.**
- Login's filled/ready state is **not an authenticated-success or username-availability assertion**: its borders remain neutral until an actual error, and it imposes no new signup complexity checks. The error sample retains the generic **Incorrect username or password.** under the password field with a red border, not a disclosure of which credential exists. A true successful login navigates to Lessons rather than showing a green credential-success screen. All sample states are illustrative; no real credentials/account/availability request is used.
- Approved pale static login background and fog signup background are reused with cover cropping; white forms and neutral controls remain readable. Both mobile forms fill the scenery across the full background with no letterboxing; long forms scroll.
